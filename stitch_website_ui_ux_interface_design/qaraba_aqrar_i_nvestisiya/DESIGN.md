## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an institutional, authoritative, yet technologically advanced agro-investment ecosystem tailored for Azerbaijan's Ministry of Agriculture, state stakeholders, and local/international investors. It projects sovereignty, agricultural renewal, ecological regeneration, and rigorous transparency.

### Core Philosophy & Visual Language
The design direction synthesizes **Institutional Modernism** with **Agro-Tech Functionalism**. It avoids bureaucratic stagnation by incorporating crisp, data-dense interfaces, tactile precision, and purposeful negative space. 
- **Institutional Weight**: Grounded structure, clean geometric divisions, and authoritative typography convey state-level security and legislative trust (aligned with EKTİS and state digital standards).
- **Regenerative Vitality**: Deep forest greens and warm mineral sands evoke fertile Karabakh soil, terraced valley topography, and high-tech agrarian revitalization.
- **Precision Density**: Layouts favor high-utility data tables, telemetry readouts, spatial cadastral mappings (GIS), and clear visual status indicators that reduce cognitive burden for both ministerial evaluators and private applicants.

## Colors

The palette balances the organic fertility of liberated Karabakh with the sovereign dignity and digital reliability of state technical platforms.

### Primary (`#0D4D3A` — Deep Emerald Forest)
The flagship identity color. Represents revitalized soil, sustainable agro-tech, and national reconstruction. Used for primary CTAs, active navigation states, key application stages, and header framing.

### Secondary (`#1B3B6F` — Caspian Cobalt)
Represents institutional governance, legislative rigor, ministry standards, and technological trust. Used for analytical charts, secondary actions, cadastral parcel selections, and EKTİS integration anchors.

### Tertiary (`#C59B27` — Khari Bulbul Gold / Wheat)
Serves as an intentional accent for priority notifications, strategic tender badges, investment commitments, and milestone verification states.

### Neutral System & Surfaces
- **Canvas / Substrate (`#F4F6F3`)**: Warm mineral sand and limestone tone, softening harsh monitor glare in extended administrative sessions.
- **Elevated Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure, crisp white cards and data sheets for optimal contrast against analytical layers.
- **Border Definers (`#D8DFD5`)**: Subtle vegetative-slate borders providing crisp physical structure without visual clutter.
- **Deep Slate Charcoal (`#111A16`)**: Replaces standard black for typography, reducing eye strain while ensuring absolute WCAG AAA legibility.

### Semantic Status Tokens
- **Qəbul edilib (Received / Active)**: Surface `#E7F4EE`, Border `#94D3B4`, Text `#085D3A`
- **Baxılır (In Review / Processing)**: Surface `#EBF2FC`, Border `#A4C6F5`, Text `#144B9E`
- **Əlavə məlumat tələb olunur (Action Required)**: Surface `#FFF7E6`, Border `#FFD591`, Text `#B76E00`
- **İmtina (Rejected)**: Surface `#FDECEE`, Border `#F8A5AC`, Text `#A81C28`
- **Qərar verilib / Təsdiqləndi (Approved / Finalized)**: Surface `#D4EDDA`, Border `#7BC68D`, Text `#0F5132`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural headlines with **Inter** for dense transactional UI, forms, and analytical readouts.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans**: Delivers contemporary authority, clarity, and balanced proportions across ministerial announcements, stage indicators, and top-level KPI metrics.
- **Inter**: Selected for its exceptional legibility in localized Azerbaijani characters (`ə`, `ı`, `ö`, `ğ`, `ç`, `ş`), dense cadastral registries, and multi-tier data tables.
- **Tabular Figures**: All data-dense components (financial amounts in AZN, land hectares `ha`, application tracking numbers) must enforce monospace/tabular numbering (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to maintain vertical alignment in tables and financial ledgers.

## Layout & Spacing

The platform is designed around a structured 12-column layout built for simultaneous high-density spatial inspection and procedural documentation.

### Grid Anatomy
- **Desktop (1280px and above)**: 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, fluid container with a maximum content constraint of `1440px`. Dedicated 280px collapsible navigation rail for administrative workflows.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 24px side canvas margins. GIS viewers stack beneath summary dossiers or adapt to split-screen toggles.
- **Mobile (Below 768px)**: 4 columns, 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters, 16px (`1rem`) outer margins. Multi-step wizards convert into pinned sticky progress headers with vertical linear panels.

### Split Layout for GIS & Dossiers
For tender and parcel management screens, the viewport supports a functional 50/50 or 60/40 asynchronous split:
- **Left Panel (Spatial Cadastre)**: Interactive vector map showing parcel zones (Ağdam, Füzuli, Şuşa, Cəbrayıl, etc.) with elevation, soil analysis, and infrastructure layers.
- **Right Panel (Investment Dossier)**: Scrollable, high-density metric summary, financial terms, downloadable legal packs, and application initiation controls.

## Elevation & Depth

To uphold an institutional and reliable tone, this design system avoids heavy shadows, blur gimmicks, and high-gloss neomorphism. Depth is achieved via **tonal layering**, **structural surface separation**, and **tinted grounding shadows**.

### Elevation Tiers
1. **Flat / Base Layer (`Level 0`)**: The sand-tinted canvas `#F4F6F3`. Forms the unified foundational canvas behind all split layouts and dashboard frames.
2. **Surface Card (`Level 1`)**: `#FFFFFF` background bound by a crisp border `1px solid #D8DFD5`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(13, 77, 58, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(17, 26, 22, 0.03)`.
3. **Elevated / Interactive Panel (`Level 2`)**: Active evaluation cards, open dropdown lists, and hover states. Shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(13, 77, 58, 0.08), 0 2px 4px rgba(17, 26, 22, 0.04)`. Border shifts slightly darker to `#C2CCC0`.
4. **Overlay / Modals & GIS Floating Toolbars (`Level 3`)**: Filter drawer menus, polygon draw tooltips, and confirmation dialogues. Shadow: `0 12px 32px rgba(17, 26, 22, 0.12), 0 4px 8px rgba(17, 26, 22, 0.06)`. Accompanied by a 24% dark slate backdrop overlay (`rgba(17, 26, 22, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(2px)`).

## Shapes

The platform adopts a controlled, semi-soft geometric profile (`roundedness: 1`). It evokes technological structure, architectural resilience, and ministerial authority without appearing harsh or brutalist.

- **Standard Inputs, Badges, Table Rows**: `0.25rem` (4px). Clean, precise, and structurally sharp.
- **Cards, Modals, GIS Containers**: `0.5rem` (8px). Softens large modules without sacrificing internal real estate.
- **Pills / Status Chips**: `9999px` strictly for semantic process status tags, distinguishing them visually from actionable rectangular buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Müsabiqəyə müraciət et / Təsdiqlə)**: `#0D4D3A` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `font-weight: 600`, 4px corner radius. On hover: shifts to `#093729`. Active: subtle inset border.
- **Secondary Ministry Action (EKTİS-dən yüklə / Yadda saxla)**: Border `1.5px solid #1B3B6F`, text `#1B3B6F`, transparent background. On hover: background `#F0F4FA`.
- **Destructive Action (Müraciəti ləğv et)**: Border `1px solid #F8A5AC`, background `#FDF4F4`, text `#A81C28`.

### Status Badges (Vəziyyət Göstəriciləri)
Rendered as pill shapes with a 1px matching border and dedicated dot indicator:
- **Qəbul edilib**: Emerald dot, `#E7F4EE` fill, `#085D3A` text.
- **Baxılır**: Cobalt dot, `#EBF2FC` fill, `#144B9E` text.
- **Əlavə məlumat tələb olunur**: Amber dot, `#FFF7E6` fill, `#B76E00` text.
- **Qərar verilib**: Deep green check-dot, `#D4EDDA` fill, `#0F5132` text.

### Data Tables
- Header: `#ECF1EC` surface, `#4A5851` uppercase `label-sm` text, bottom border `2px solid #D8DFD5`.
- Rows: `#FFFFFF` alternate with `#FAFBF9`, height 52px, cell padding `12px 16px`, hover state `#F2F7F4`.
- Integrated sorting indicators, inline status chips, and sticky right-hand action cells (Dossier review, PDF export).

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Base Input**: `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #D8DFD5` border, 8px padding vertical, 12px horizontal.
- **Focus State**: `1px solid #0D4D3A` with an ambient outer glow `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(13, 77, 58, 0.12)`.
- **Validation**: Embedded inline feedback icons; red `#A81C28` for missing cadastral documentation, green `#085D3A` for verified ASAN İmza signatures.

### Multi-Step Application Wizard (Müraciət Addımları)
Pinned stepper showing stage indicators:
1. *İnvestor Məlumatları* (Investor Credentials)
2. *Aqrar Layihə Planı* (Agro-Project Proposal)
3. *Torpaq və Ərazi Seçimi* (Cadastral Parcel Selection via GIS)
4. *Maliyyə Əsaslandırması* (Financial Justification)
5. *ASAN İmza ilə Təsdiq* (Digital Verification & Submission)
Completed steps turn forest green with a check icon; active step is indicated by a deep cobalt border with white center; upcoming steps remain neutral slate.
